The problem

Every teacher with IEP or 504 students has to show they delivered what the plan promised. Most do it in a notebook, a spreadsheet, or from memory at the end of a long week. Then a meeting is called and the evidence has to be assembled from nothing.

How it works

1

Set up once

Add your students, their class periods, and the accommodations each plan calls for.

2

Two minutes a day

One board, one lane per student. Mark what you delivered and close out the day.

3

Print the evidence

A dated report per student or per period, ready for a meeting or a file.

Nothing leaves your computer

This is the part that matters most, because Bloom documents disabled children. There is no account, no database and no cloud. Everything lives in one file on your machine, in a folder you choose.

The part of the app that holds student data physically cannot reach the network. It is blocked at the browser engine level, not by a policy or a promise, and the code that does it is public and readable. The only connection Bloom ever makes is a check for a newer version, which carries no data about you and can be switched off permanently in Settings.
